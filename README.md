# AI Product Manager Copilot

An AI-powered Product Management Assistant that helps product teams accelerate the creation and refinement of product artifacts using Generative AI.

The AI Product Manager Copilot streamlines key product management activities by generating Product Requirement Documents (PRDs), creating user stories, recommending roadmap priorities, and refining product backlogs. It enables Product Managers, Product Owners, Business Analysts, and Digital Transformation teams to focus more on strategy and customer value while reducing time spent on repetitive documentation tasks.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Product teams spend significant time documenting requirements, maintaining backlogs, and aligning roadmaps with business objectives. AI Product Manager Copilot leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) to automate and enhance these activities.

The solution transforms product ideas, stakeholder inputs, business goals, and market insights into structured product deliverables while maintaining consistency and best practices.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

### 📄 PRD Generation

Generate comprehensive Product Requirement Documents from high-level business objectives or product concepts.

**Capabilities**
- Problem statement generation
- Business objectives definition
- User persona identification
- Functional requirements creation
- Non-functional requirements generation
- Success metrics recommendation
- Risk and dependency identification

---

### 📝 User Story Writing

Convert requirements and product ideas into actionable Agile user stories.

**Capabilities**
- User story generation
- Acceptance criteria creation
- INVEST-compliant stories
- Epic decomposition
- Story prioritization
- Agile-ready formats

**Example**

> As a customer,
> I want to receive real-time order notifications,
> so that I can track my purchases effectively.

---

### 🗺️ Roadmap Recommendations

Generate data-driven roadmap suggestions based on product strategy, customer needs, and business priorities.

**Capabilities**
- Strategic initiative identification
- Feature prioritization
- Impact vs. effort analysis
- Quarter-based planning
- Product goal alignment
- Theme-based roadmap generation

---

### 📋 Backlog Refinement

Improve backlog quality and readiness for development teams.

**Capabilities**
- Duplicate detection
- Story enhancement
- Missing acceptance criteria identification
- Priority recommendations
- Dependency analysis
- Sprint readiness assessment
